# Project Scope — Smart Inventory Firaza

## Business context
Firaza is a retail business focused on hampers and personalized gifts. The project addresses manual monitoring of packaging materials and supporting materials.

## MVP scope
- Material master data (create, read, update, delete with transaction-history protection).
- Stock-in, stock-out, and stock opname recording.
- Current stock, minimum stock, transaction history, and usage features.
- ML classification: Safe, Need Attention, Restock Priority.
- REST inference API.
- Generative AI inventory summary and restock recommendations.

## Explicit limitations
- Inventory and usage data are synthetic demonstration data, not actual Firaza operational data.
- Marketplace sales integration is excluded.
- ML labels are generated by a transparent rule-based simulation; evaluation measures agreement with that synthetic rule, not real-world business accuracy.
- AI recommendations are decision support and require human validation.
